High Jump Analyzer

Upload a clip of one jump. The app marks your joints, measures your angles and rates the jump against targets for your height and gender. Add the bar height to check your vertical jump against the bar and whether every body part cleared it.

How to film for the best result
  • Film from the takeoff side with the bar running across the picture, so the camera sees the last steps, the takeoff and the bar.
  • Get both bar ends in frame: the app finds the bar and uses your bar height as the scale for every measurement.
  • Keep the whole body in frame from the last 4-5 steps through the landing, and fill as much of the picture as you can.
  • Hold the phone still (a tripod helps), do not zoom, and film in good light.
  • Slow-motion clips work: set Clip speed to match (most phones film slow-mo at 4× or 8×).
  • One jump per clip. Up to 15 seconds is analyzed from the start time.
