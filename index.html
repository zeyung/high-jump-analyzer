High Jump Analyzer

Upload a clip of one jump. The app marks your joints, measures your angles and rates the jump against targets for your height and gender. Add the bar height to check your vertical jump against the bar and whether every body part cleared it.

How to film for the best result
  • Film from the takeoff side with the bar running across the picture, so the camera sees the last steps, the takeoff and the bar.
  • Get both bar ends in frame: you mark the bar before tracking, and your bar height becomes the scale for every measurement.
  • A still camera on a tripod is best. If you pan to follow the jumper, the app corrects for it, but smooth slow pans work best.
  • Keep the whole body in frame from the last 4-5 steps through the landing, and fill as much of the picture as you can.
  • Do not zoom during the jump, and film in good light.
  • Slow-motion clips work: set Clip speed to match (most phones film slow-mo at 4× or 8×).
  • One jump per clip. Up to 15 seconds is analyzed from the start time.

Pick the jumper and the bar

  1. Use the slider to find a frame where the jumper is clearly visible, then tap the jumper.
  2. Optional: tap the jumper again on a frame where they are over the bar. It keeps tracking on the jumper through the flight.
  3. Tap the two ends of the bar, then the floor directly under it. Your bar height becomes the measuring stick for the jump.
