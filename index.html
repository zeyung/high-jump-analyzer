High Jump Analyzer

Upload a clip of one jump. The app marks your joints, measures your angles and rates the jump against targets for your height and gender. Add the bar height to see if your hips cleared it.

How to film for the best result
  • Film from the side, roughly in line with the bar, so the camera sees the curve and the takeoff side-on.
  • Keep the whole body in frame from the last 4-5 steps through the landing.
  • Hold the phone still (a tripod helps) and do not zoom during the jump.
  • One jump per clip. Up to 15 seconds is analyzed from the start time.
